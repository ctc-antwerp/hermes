# Privacy Policy - Hermes persoonlijk

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Hermes persoonlijk is a private assistant used only by its owner, Tarik Mohandis (CTC Antwerp, Belgium), with his own Google account. It is not offered to other users.

## Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app accesses Gmail (read, label, archive, move to trash, create labels and filters), Google Calendar events (create and edit) and Google Drive files.

## How data is used

Data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests, such as sorting email, managing appointments and organising files. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties, except the AI model providers the owner chooses to process his own requests.

## Storage and security

OAuth tokens and data are stored only on systems controlled by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
